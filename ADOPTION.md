# Pilot and evidence protocol

1. Identify a consenting independent pilot organization and a real problem. Obtain permission before naming it.
2. Record the existing approach, workload, environment, baseline and acceptance criteria before testing.
3. Use a sandbox or authorized offline export with synthetic/redacted data. Keep private records outside this repo.
4. Pin the evaluated commit, record configuration, failures, throughput/latency where relevant, and limitations.
5. Repeat on the same workload with the comparison method. Publish reproducible scripts; report adverse results too.
6. Record what was actually adopted, deployment dates, users, integration scope and maintainers' issues or PRs.
7. Ask users for factual feedback in their own words: what they used, why, measurable results and evidence sources.

No fabricated testimonials, purchased engagement, reciprocal star campaigns or backdated records.
A pilot does not automatically demonstrate significance beyond that organization. Independent review is welcome.
